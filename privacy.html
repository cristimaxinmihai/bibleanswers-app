Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This policy explains what BibleAnswers collects when you use the site, why, and what choices you have. BibleAnswers is operated by an individual based in the United States. Contact: cristimaxinmihai@yahoo.com.

The short version. You can ask one question a day without an account. After that, asking questions requires a free account, which needs only an email address and a password. We don't ask for your name, and we don't sell your data. Your questions are sent to an AI provider so it can answer them. We use analytics tools that record how pages are used — including a session-replay tool configured so that all text on the page is masked before it leaves your browser. If you subscribe, Stripe handles your payment and we never see your card number.

1. What we collect

Your account

To ask questions you create an account with an email address and a password. Passwords are handled and stored in hashed form by Supabase, our authentication provider; we never see them in readable form. Linked to your account we also store: the number of questions you have asked today and the date of that count, your subscription status, the end date of your current subscription period, and a customer identifier supplied by Stripe.

Questions you submit

The text of each question you ask is transmitted to our server and forwarded to our AI provider to generate an answer. Your questions are not saved to your account after the answer is returned. Please avoid including sensitive personal details — health information, financial account numbers, government identifiers — in your questions.

Analytics and session recording

We use four analytics tools to understand how the site is used and where it is confusing or broken.

  • Vercel Web Analytics records page views and general traffic patterns. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you across websites.
  • Microsoft Clarity records anonymised session replays and heatmaps: mouse movement, clicks, taps, scrolling, page transitions, and general device and browser information. Clarity also sets its own first-party cookies to recognise a returning session.
  • Google Analytics records page views, approximate location at country and city level, device and browser type, and which site or platform you arrived from. Unlike the two tools above, Google Analytics places cookies in your browser to recognise returning visits. We use it only in aggregate, to see how many people find the site and where they come from.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) records page views and whether a question was asked, so that we can measure the results of our advertising. Like Google Analytics, it places cookies in your browser, and Meta may connect this activity to your Facebook or Instagram account.

Clarity is configured in its strict masking mode. This means the text on the page — including anything you type into the chat box and the answers you receive — is replaced with blocks before the recording leaves your browser. We can see that a visitor typed a question and scrolled through an answer; we cannot read what the question or the answer said. Passwords and other input fields are masked in the same way.

These recordings are used only to improve the Service. We do not use them for advertising and we do not attempt to identify individual visitors from them.

Information stored in your own browser

After you sign in, a session token is stored locally in your browser (using local storage, not tracking cookies) so that you stay signed in between visits. Signing out or clearing your browser data removes it. Your free-trial and daily-question counts are not kept in your browser — they are stored on our server and tied to your account.

Payment information

If you subscribe, Stripe collects and processes your payment details directly. We receive only limited information from Stripe, such as your subscription status, billing email, and the last four digits and brand of your card. We never receive or store your full card number.

Technical information

Our hosting provider automatically logs standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps. This is used to keep the site running securely and to diagnose errors.

Asking without an account

You can ask one question per day without creating an account. To apply that limit we store a hashed form of your IP address together with a date and a count. We do not store the IP address itself for this purpose, and the record is not linked to any account, question, or answer. Once you create an account, your usage is counted against the account instead.

2. What we do not collect

  • No name, mailing address, or phone number.
  • We never send the text of your questions or answers to advertising platforms.
  • No readable copy of what you type, in any session recording.

3. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • To generate answers to your questions.
  • To enforce free trial periods and daily question limits.
  • To process subscriptions, renewals, and refunds.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and keep the Service secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. How long we keep it

Your account information is kept for as long as your account exists. You may ask us to delete your account and the data attached to it at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep records for tax or legal reasons. Server logs are retained for a limited period by our hosting provider and then deleted or rotated. Session recordings and analytics data are retained by our analytics providers according to their own retention periods and are then deleted automatically. Questions sent to our AI provider are retained according to that provider's own retention practices. Billing records are kept by Stripe as long as required by tax and accounting law.

6. Local storage and cookies

BibleAnswers uses browser local storage to keep you signed in, as described above. Microsoft Clarity sets its own first-party cookies so that it can recognise a continuing session and distinguish new visitors from returning ones. Vercel Web Analytics does not set cookies. Stripe may set its own cookies during checkout for fraud prevention and payment security. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not allow third parties to build advertising profiles from your use of this site.

You can block or delete these cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Clarity by using your browser's Do Not Track or equivalent privacy settings, or by using an ad- and tracker-blocking extension. Blocking them does not affect your ability to use BibleAnswers.

7. Children's privacy

BibleAnswers is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Our legal bases for processing are performance of a contract (providing the Service you requested) and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and understanding and improving how the Service is used).

Everyone

To exercise any of these rights, including deletion of your account, email cristimaxinmihai@yahoo.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

We operate from the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. If you use the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit and rely on established providers for hosting and payments. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
