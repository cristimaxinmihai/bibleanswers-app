Editorial Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

BibleAnswers exists to help people find Scripture and think through questions of faith. This page explains where the answers on this site come from, so you can weigh them properly.

Two kinds of answers

The site contains two distinct types of content, and they are not produced the same way:

  • Written answers. The questions and answers listed under Common Questions & Answers, and everything reachable from the footer, are written and reviewed in advance by the site operator before publication.
  • AI chat answers. Responses in the chat are generated on demand by an AI language model. They are not reviewed by a person before you see them.

AI-generated answers can be incomplete or wrong. Weigh anything you read here against Scripture itself, and bring significant questions to your pastor or church community.

Scripture quotations

Verses on this site are quoted from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. Where a passage is difficult in older English, the surrounding explanation aims to make the sense plain rather than to replace the text.

What this site does not do

  • It does not speak for any denomination, church, or ministry.
  • It does not offer counselling, medical advice, legal advice, or crisis support. If you are in danger or in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.
  • It does not attempt to settle every disputed question. Where Christians have long disagreed, the aim is to say so honestly rather than to pick a side quietly.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, a misquoted verse, or an answer that misrepresents what Scripture says, please write to cristimaxinmihai@yahoo.com. Corrections to written answers are made directly on the page.

Independence

BibleAnswers is funded by reader subscriptions. It does not accept payment to feature, recommend, or slant content, and no answer is written or altered in exchange for money.
